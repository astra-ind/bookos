Pricing

Pay for the workshop,
not the words.

BookOS is software you own, running on your machine. Intelligence comes from providers you connect yourself — we never resell model access, so AI costs are whatever your own keys cost.

A note on prices

Exact pricing isn't finalized, so this page shows plans and what they'll include rather than invented numbers. The current release is free while development continues. When paid plans arrive, they will be configured here and in the app — and your books, being plain folders on your disk, are never held hostage by a plan.
