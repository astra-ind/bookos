POV Maya · The Railway Station · Day 42, evening

The platform emptied the way it always did — not at once, but in the manner of a tide deciding to withdraw. Maya kept her place on the bench and watched the last light go amber against the ironwork.

In her coat pocket, the letter had gone soft at the folds. She had read it eleven times and had still not decided what it meant — only what it demanded.

The 6:40 came through without stopping. Arun would not be on it. She had known that before she came, and she had come anyway.
